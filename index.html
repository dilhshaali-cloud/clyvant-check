How it’s worked out

  1. Kept before ads = paid out by TikTok − VAT inside sales − product cost − free samples
  2. Kept per €1 of sales = kept ÷ shop sales
  3. Real ROI = TikTok’s ROI × kept per €1 of sales
  4. Break-even ROI = 1 ÷ kept per €1 of sales

TikTok’s payout already has its fees, creator commission, shipping, sample shipping and refunds taken out. The Finance page counts payouts by the day TikTok paid, not the day of the order. Over a few months of steady sales that evens out. If your sales grew fast, your real ROI is a little better than shown.
