How the math works
  1. Credit. Gross revenue in Shop Ads ÷ GMV in Shop Analytics. While a GMV Max campaign runs, TikTok counts every order for the products in it as an ad order, even if the buyer never saw an ad (TikTok help centre).
  2. Kept before ads. Exact: what TikTok paid you for those dates, plus what’s on hold, minus VAT and product cost. Quick: GMV minus cancellations and refunds, VAT, TikTok fees, creator commission, shipping and product cost.
  3. It really brought back. Gross revenue × (kept before ads ÷ GMV). GMV Max gets every sale TikTok credits it with.
  4. Real ROI = that ÷ ad cost. Break-even ROI = GMV ÷ kept before ads: the ROI TikTok has to show before ads pay for themselves.
  5. Verdict. Scale when ads keep 0.30 or more per 1 spent after every cost. Keep between 0 and 0.30. Cut below 0.
  6. TikTok’s GMV already includes what buyers pay for shipping, and doesn’t subtract cancellations or refunds (TikTok’s own definition in Shop Analytics).
