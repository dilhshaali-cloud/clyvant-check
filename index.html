How it’s worked out

Four steps, the same for every shop and market.

Credit
Gross revenue divided by GMV. TikTok counts every order for a campaign’s products as an ad order, seen or not (help centre).
Kept before ads
Your payout for those dates, minus VAT and product cost.
Real ROI
What the credited sales kept, divided by ad cost.
Break-even ROI
The ROI TikTok has to show before your ads pay for themselves.
